The agreement, in plain English

How long

5 years, with the option to renew for another 5 if it’s working for you.

Your way out

A break clause: 12 months’ notice, any time after year 3.

At the end

We remove everything and make good your playground, at our cost.

Cost to your school

None. We pay for upkeep, insurance and our electricity, on its own meter.

Annual review

The school day is yours. Each year we review how it’s going and agree the next timetable together.

Responsibility

We insure, maintain and inspect it, and cover our own sessions.

Safeguarding

Separate access out of hours, agreed with your DSL.

No surprises

Nothing is binding until planning and your governors’ or trust’s consent are in place.

Headline terms · final wording agreed by both sides’ solicitors · 14

The headline terms a head and their governors will want before anything else. The 5-year term matches the finance on the canopy and containers, and the kind of time-limited planning consent temporary structures often get. The earliest a school can leave is the end of year 4, so at most a year of finance is uncovered, on kit that can move to another school. Final wording to settle with our solicitor.
