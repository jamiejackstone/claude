The road to opening

1

Discovery call

Twenty minutes: your school, your site and what you’d love it to do.

2

Site visit

Thirty minutes on your playground: space, access and power.

3

Agree in principle

A short, non-binding letter, with a pack for your governors.

4

Planning and consents

Our team prepares, submits and pays for everything.

5

Install

About a week, in the school holidays.

6

Open

Your Heroes Arena, ready for your pupils and community.

Every step depends on planning permission and the landowner’s consent.

Close on the smallest possible ask: a 20-minute discovery call, then a 30-minute site visit. The letter is non-binding; its job is to let us start pre-application planning advice and the consent process with the trust or local authority. The school is never committed to anything before consents are in place, and installing in the holidays keeps disruption to a minimum.
