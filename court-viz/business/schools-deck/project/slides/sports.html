Basketball runs as a mini court across the width or as 3x3 on one hoop, which also suits wheelchair basketball; sports wheelchairs are kept in the storage. Each sport has its own colour, on the lines and the boards around the court. Colour-coded lines, portable nets and rolling hoops mean the court changes activity in minutes. Music, when a session needs it, comes from a portable speaker.
