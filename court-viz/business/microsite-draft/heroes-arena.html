Heroes Arena

Your home court

A covered, all-weather sports arena on your school playground. Free to your school, run by Hoop Heroes: your pupils have it all day, and we bring the community in after school, at weekends and in the holidays.

What goes on your playground

  • Footprint: about 22 × 12 m (270 m², 2,900 sq ft), roughly the size of a tennis court
  • Covered, enclosed and LED lit, with a 14.8 × 10 m court
  • Sound system for music, dance and fitness
  • Dedicated, secure storage for all the kit
  • Reception and viewing area with an accessible WC, step-free onto the court
  • No digging or building work: it sits on your playground
  • About a week to install, in the school holidays
  • Fully removable, playground made good

One space, many sports

Basketball is our flagship. Roll the hoops away and the same court hosts pickleball and dodgeball, plus dance, fitness and martial arts. Every sport has its own colour, on the court lines and the boards round the walls.

Built for everyone

Level, step-free access onto the court, an accessible WC in the reception, and sports wheelchairs kept in the storage, so wheelchair basketball is part of the week, not an add-on.

What your site needs

Space
A flat, hard area of about 22 × 12 m, such as a playground, MUGA or car park, with a little room around it.
Surface
Tarmac or concrete, roughly level. No digging or foundations: the containers sit on top.
Height
About 8 m clear overhead: no cables, and any trees trimmed back.
Access
Room for a lorry and a small crane on install day, and a route families can use out of hours without going through the school.
Power, water and drainage
A power supply nearby, on our own meter, and a water and drain connection for the WC, or we use a self-contained unit.

The agreement, in plain English

How long
5 years, with the option to renew for another 5 if it's working for you.
Your way out
A break clause: 12 months' notice, any time after year 3.
Annual review
The school day is yours. Each year we review how it's going and agree the next timetable together.
Cost and responsibility
None to you. We insure, maintain and inspect it, and pay for our electricity on its own meter.
At the end, and no surprises
We remove everything and make good your playground, at our cost. Nothing is binding until planning and your governors' or trust's consent are in place.

Your questions, answered

Do we need planning permission?+

Yes, and our team handles all of it: drawings, application and fees, at our cost.

Will it disrupt the school?+

We install in the school holidays. No digging, no concrete, no building work.

Who uses it, and when?+

Your school, all day. We run sessions in the evenings, at weekends and in the holidays.

Who is responsible out of hours?+

We are: our staff and our insurance, with separate access agreed with your DSL and kept apart from school buildings.

What's the catch?+

None. We fund it because we use it outside school hours. Everyone wins.

What if it doesn't work out?+

We take it away and make good your playground, at our cost.
