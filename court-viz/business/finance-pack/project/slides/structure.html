Decided 29 Oct 2026: Arena #1 runs through Hoop Heroes Region 1 Ltd; this two-company structure is for later, once Arena #1 is proven. Questions for the accountant then: the CIC asset lock, VAT on sports-facility hire, Flat Rate VAT limits for the Ltd, and related-party rules. A proposal for the accountant and solicitor, not advice. The school licenses the site to whichever body holds the Arena, with a clause allowing assignment to the CIC later. The fees between the two companies must be at arm's length: a below-market fee could breach the CIC's asset lock, and Jamie would sit on both sides, so the CIC needs a conflicts policy and possibly an independent director. KB decision D1 is still open: run Arena #1 through the Ltd first and form the CIC when a named grant needs it. The CIC side is also what gives partners and funders a clear community story.
