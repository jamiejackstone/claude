What could go wrong

Families don’t move

Survey Wendover families first; keep the halls during the switch. The downside case models this.

Growth is slower

The cautious case dips below halls in Year 1, then reaches over four times today by Year 3. Gate signing on a waitlist and pre-registrations.

Build costs rise

The canopy quote came in at £87k, more than double the early budget. 10% contingency; rent the containers; reception can be a Year 2 upgrade.

Finance and personal exposure

Accountant review first; avoid a personal guarantee; insist on relocation and early-exit terms.

Planning or the school changes

Pre-application advice; a 5-year licence with break and removal clauses; the Arena can move.

Grants don’t land

Arena #1 needs none: it runs through the Ltd. Grants only start to matter for Arenas #2 and #3.
