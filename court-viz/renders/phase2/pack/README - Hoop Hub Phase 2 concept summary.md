# Hoop Hub Phase 2: concept summary

**Concept only. Not a real install, site or approved plan.** Phase 2 encloses the Phase 1 Hoop Hub court under a canopy so it can be used all year. Phase 1 (the open court with the 10 ft "hub in a box" container) is unchanged and has its own pack.

## The idea in one line
The Phase 1 court sits inside a translucent white PVC arch carried on two rows of containers: a 40 ft reception on one side, two 20 ft storage units on the other.

## Layout (as agreed, 21 Oct 2026)
- **Footprint:** 18.0 m clear between the container faces × 12.19 m long (the length of a 40 ft container). About 22.9 m wide overall.
- **Court:** the Phase 1 court, unchanged (14.8 × 10.0 m, white 3x3 and gold mini markings, Mega Slam 72 and two portable hoops), centred inside.
- **Run-off:** same grey panels as the court. 1.6 m each side, 1.1 m at each end.
- **Arch:** springs from the container roofs (2.59 m), crown about 7.6 m, steel frames inside, 12 LED high-bay lights.
- **End walls:** fabric, navy to about 6 m with a gold stripe, white above. No gaps at the corners: the end walls sit flush with the container ends.
- **Doors:** one main roller door, 6.0 × 4.5 m, centred in the entrance end wall. One fire exit, 1.0 × 2.1 m, in the Mega Slam end wall right of the hoop, with a lit green exit sign.

## Containers
- **Container 1 – reception (40 ft, left):** glazed along the court side with a glazed door, so parents can watch. Inside: WC at the Mega Slam end, desk, seating and a heater. Three parents' benches along the glazing on the court side.
- **Containers 2 and 3 – storage (20 ft each, right):** each has one 2.3 m roller shutter on the court side at its corner end, clear of the right portable hoop. Ball racks are kept inside. Container 2 is navy, container 3 is gold. Their exact use is not fixed yet.

## Branding
- **Inside:** Hoop Heroes logo and round badges on the wall behind the Mega Slam; logos on the court side of containers 2 and 3; logo above the door on the entrance wall; RECEPTION sign and logo safety markings on the glazing.
- **Outside:** HOOP HUB above the roller door with a logo either side; big logo and HOOP HUB on the long sides of containers 1 and 2; "Scan to book a FREE trial" QR panel on container 3 (placeholder link: hoopheroes.co.uk); round badges on the container ends.
- Brand: navy #14264e and gold #fba91a; Lilita One (headings, upper case) and Montserrat. "Hoop Heroes" always two words.

## Changes from the Phase 2 brief v3
The brief v3 plan and elevations are superseded on these points:
- Length cut from about 14 m to 12.19 m. The 0.9 m fabric gap panels at the corners are removed.
- Fire exits: FE2 is removed. One exit only, FE1 in the Mega Slam end wall.
- Run-off is grey, not navy-grey.
- Storage doors are one roller shutter per container at the corner end.
- The containers are named Container 1 (reception), 2 and 3. No "school storage" naming for now.

## Still open
- The 1.1 m run-off behind each baseline is tight. It needs checking against guidance for a real build.
- The number and position of fire exits needs a fire risk assessment and supplier sign-off.
- The arch structure, its fixing to the containers and the ballast are visual concept only. A supplier needs to confirm them.
- The QR code needs a real booking link.
