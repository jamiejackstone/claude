# Hoop Hub Phase 1: concept summary

**Concept only. Not a real install, site or approved plan.** Hoop Heroes (UK children's basketball, ages 5 to 15) wants to show schools what a "hub in a box" court would look like, to use for sessions in May and June.

## The idea in one line
A permanent Mega Slam hoop goes in first. A branded 10 ft container is then dropped behind it, holding everything else: the floor, two portable hoops and the balls. Roll it out and play; pack it away the same way.

## The court
- **Floor:** 14.8 m wide × 10.0 m deep, made of 60 large modular panels (1.23 × 2.0 m, 12 across × 5 deep), laid on school playground tarmac. Mid grey panels, lighter grey keys.
- **Primary: 3x3 half court (white lines).** The 14.8 m side is the baseline, with the in-ground Mega Slam 72 centred behind it. Standard FIBA key, free-throw line and semicircle, 6.75 m arc. No no-charge semicircle.
- **Secondary: mini basketball court (gold lines), running across at 90°.** The 3x3 sidelines are its baselines, with a portable hoop at each end. Gold boundary just outside the white one, plus a key (light grey fill, gold outline), free-throw semicircle and small 3-point line (4.0 m radius) at each end. No halfway line or centre circle.
- **Logo:** Hoop Heroes landscape logo painted at the open end, left of centre.

## The hoops
- **Mega Slam 72:** in-ground, glass board, navy padding, height-adjustable 6 to 10 ft. Used for 3x3.
- **2 × Tarmak B900 portable hoops:** wheeled, fold flat, 2.4 to 3.05 m rim height. Used for the mini court, one at each end. All backboards carry the round Hoop Heroes logo.

## The container (10 ft: 2.99 × 2.44 × 2.59 m)
- **Wrap:** navy with a gold stripe. Huge Hoop Heroes logo with "HOOP HUB" in large gold letters on both long sides. Doors: QR code ("Scan to book a FREE trial") and the round logo.
- **As delivered:** loaded panel trolley by the doors; both folded hoops and a gold ball rack behind it; full-width ramp at the doors.
- **Everyday use:** empty trolley stood on its end at the back, ball rack in front, hoops by the doors when not out.

## Installation (three steps)
1. Mega Slam installed (separate, permanent).
2. Container opens; trolley rolls to the court corner; panels laid row by row from the Mega Slam end.
3. Trolley moves aside; portable hoops roll out to each end and go up; empty trolley goes back in on its end.

## Brand
Navy #14264e and gold #fba91a; Lilita One (headings, upper case) and Montserrat. "Hoop Heroes" always two words.
