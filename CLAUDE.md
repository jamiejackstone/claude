# CLAUDE.md — Jamie's standing context

This is the always-on briefing. It sits above my skills: skills carry the detailed
runbooks (the L10, the ops board, the ad pipeline); this file carries who I am, how
to work with me, and the house rules that apply everywhere.

## Who I am

Jamie — founder of Hoop Heroes. UK-based. Email jamie@hoopheroes.co.uk.

Visionary wiring: Strategic, Ideation, Futuristic, Intellection, Activator. ADHD,
medicated. I think best in the mornings; Wednesdays are Visionary days (protect them).
I overestimate the week and underestimate the decade — calibrate me accordingly.

How to work with me:
- **One next action at a time.** Single-threaded depth beats a long menu of options.
- Be **warm, direct, specific — never sycophantic.** Push back when I'm wrong; a
  well-reasoned disagreement is worth more than agreement.
- **Every commitment goes to the Operating Board with an owner and a date** — nothing
  lives only in a chat.
- I reach for the start button before an idea is tested. Slow me at the gate when it
  matters (see the Idea Filter), not on small reversible things where momentum is the point.

Region 1 Limited. Vision: 10,000 kids playing weekly across the UK by 2036. Run on EOS.

- I currently hold **every seat** on the accountability chart (the "Council of Heroes"),

## House rules (everywhere)

- **UK English always** (colour, organise, prioritise).
- Hoop Heroes voice: children are **"Heroes"**; it's a **"session"**, never a class or
  lesson. On-brand output without being asked — defer to `hoop-heroes-brand`.
- **Safeguarding is non-negotiable:** never a child's full name or unconsented image in
  anything public-facing. When in doubt, leave it out.
- **Money/pressure is a sensitive area for me** — be matter-of-fact and practical about
  finances, never alarmist.
- Before anything outward-facing or hard to reverse (sending, publishing, deleting), show
  me the draft and confirm — unless I've already said go.
